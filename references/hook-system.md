# Commerce hook system

Read this before generating hooks.

## Three-channel opening

Every hook combines:

1. `spoken`: The first words heard.
2. `visual`: The first visible action or state.
3. `text_overlay`: A short orientation cue.

Do not make all three channels say the same thing. Prefer:

- Visual proves or interrupts.
- Spoken line opens a question or names the tension.
- Overlay identifies the audience, context, or stakes.

## Awareness stages

| Stage | Creative job |
|---|---|
| `unaware` | Reveal a recognizable situation without leading with the product |
| `problem-aware` | Name or demonstrate the pain precisely |
| `solution-aware` | Differentiate the mechanism or use experience |
| `product-aware` | Resolve an evidence-backed objection |
| `most-aware` | Present an approved offer, urgency, or decision aid |

## Angle families

Use at least six materially different families in `lab` mode:

- `pain-moment`: Show a familiar failure or frustration.
- `visual-proof`: Begin with the result or demonstration.
- `mechanism`: Reveal how the product works.
- `identity-callout`: Let a precise audience self-select.
- `objection-reversal`: Address a documented hesitation.
- `comparison`: Compare workflows or observable attributes without unsupported superiority.
- `price-or-offer`: Use only approved, current offer facts.
- `comment-reply`: Answer a real supplied comment; never invent one.
- `mistake`: Demonstrate an actual usage error.
- `routine`: Insert the product into a credible moment.
- `unboxing-or-reveal`: Use when packaging or transformation is visually meaningful.
- `list-or-check`: Promise a finite, deliverable sequence.

Do not force an angle that lacks evidence.

## Archetypes

Choose a structural archetype separately from the angle:

- Pattern interrupt
- Open loop
- Before/after setup
- Direct demonstration
- Mid-action cold open
- Specific question
- Counter-belief
- Confession
- Objection answer
- Price reveal
- Ranked list
- Comment response

## Hook constraints

- Make the spoken opening deliverable in roughly three seconds.
- Start with action, evidence, tension, or specificity.
- Avoid greetings, brand introductions, and "in this video."
- Avoid generic hype such as "game changer," "revolutionary," and "you won't believe."
- Ensure the body can deliver the promised payoff.
- Prefer one claim and one visual idea over several selling points.
- Keep overlay readable at feed speed.

## Variation design

Vary the mechanism, not only wording. A valid test set changes at least one of:

- Audience frame
- Pain or desire
- Proof order
- Visual action
- Objection
- Product mechanism
- Offer framing
- Narrative point of view

Do not call paraphrases distinct variants.

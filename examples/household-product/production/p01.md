## Timeline

- 0-3s: Real macro footage shows one continuous pass on a dark woven cushion. Spoken: "This cushion only looks clean." Overlay: "The hidden layer."
- 3-8s: Camera stays close as visible loose hair gathers at the tool edge. Spoken: "The surface hid more loose hair than I noticed."
- 8-12s: Open the chamber and show the real collected hair. No generated result footage.
- 12-15s: Return to the actual cushion and product. CTA: "See how it works on your fabric."

## Generic prompt

Create a 15-second 9:16 creator-style commerce video using the supplied product reference. Begin on real macro footage of a dark woven cushion as one continuous pass reveals visible loose pet hair. Keep the camera handheld but stable, use natural window light, and cut to a close view of the chamber opening. Preserve the product shape and material. Add spoken audio but no generated captions, prices, logos, or performance claims.

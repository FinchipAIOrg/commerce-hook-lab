# P03 - reusable-routine opening

Selected hook: H06  
Evidence: F03, F04, F05

## Timeline

- 0-3s: Empty disposable lint roll slides out of frame as the reusable tool enters. Spoken: "No fresh lint sheet this time." Overlay: "Use it again."
- 3-7s: Real product completes a short pass on woven fabric.
- 7-11s: Open and empty the chamber.
- 11-15s: Close it and begin a second pass. CTA: "See the reusable routine."

## Generic prompt

Create a 15-second 9:16 routine-focused commerce video using the supplied product reference. Start with an empty disposable lint roll sliding aside while the reusable handheld tool enters frame. Cut to a real short pass on woven fabric, then a close view of the chamber opening and being emptied before a second pass begins. Use an everyday home setting, direct natural light, and creator-style pacing. Preserve the exact product design. Do not imply universal superiority or generate text, price, logos, or cleaning proof.

# Audience and controlled test

Audience: problem-aware pet owners who notice loose hair on dark fabric furniture before guests arrive.

Pain: visible loose hair makes a recently cleaned room feel unfinished.

Desire: a reusable, easy-to-empty tool for routine touch-ups.

Assumption status: supplied in the owned brief, not independently researched.

Objective: improve the opening hold on a 15-second TikTok Shop video.

Changed variable: the first 0-3 second hook.

Controlled variables:

- Body demonstration
- CTA
- Duration
- Creator and voice
- Product reference
- Audience and placement
- Offer

Primary metric: 3-second hold rate.

Secondary metrics: 6-second hold rate and click-through rate.

Stopping rule: not provided. The media buyer must define minimum exposure and a stopping rule before comparing variants.
